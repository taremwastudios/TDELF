About Our Organization

Thenuel Debate and Literary Fronesis TDELF is a prominent Ugandan youth empowerment organization focused on debate, public speaking, and leadership mentorship. Founded by Executive Director Emmanuel Obore, TDELF equips young learners across the country with critical thinking and advocacy skills to drive positive social transformation.Through hands-on training and interactive programs, the organization bridges skills gaps and helps students address pressing societal issues. By collaborating with national bodies and UN agencies, TDELF instills a deep sense of civic duty and global citizenship in the next generation. Their annual calendar features major events like the Youth Debate & Literary Championship, where hundreds of students converge to compete in debates, poetry, and quizzes. TDELF also publishes literary works and trains young people to articulate complex topics such as gender-based violence and sustainable development.Their mission of refocusing youthful energies toward nation building is bolstered through strategic partnerships with organizations like Facebook and professionals on LinkedIn Uganda. By continually amplifying the voices of East Africa’s youth, TDELF serves as a dynamic catalyst for cultivating visionary future leaders.
