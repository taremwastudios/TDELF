About Us

About the Organization

Built on ambition and a passion for youth development, TDELF is committed to fostering critical thinking and leadership skills among young people in Uganda.

TDELF is a prominent Ugandan youth empowerment organization focused on debate, public speaking, and leadership mentorship. Founded by Executive Director Emmanuel Obore, TDELF equips young learners across the country with critical thinking and advocacy skills to drive positive social transformation. We live because you do, we serve, live to make you great and grow with you along the way.
